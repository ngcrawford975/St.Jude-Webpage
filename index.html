Why St. Jude Matters

St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital treats children with cancer and life‑threatening diseases at no cost to families. Their groundbreaking research is shared worldwide, helping improve treatments and outcomes everywhere.

Your support helps fund treatment, housing, meals, and research that gives children a chance to grow, heal, and thrive.

Why The Melting Pot Supports St. Jude

The Melting Pot is built on togetherness — sharing a pot, sharing a moment. St. Jude is built on community support — no child fights alone.

Every year, our guests and team unite to make a meaningful impact. We believe every shared meal can spark something bigger: comfort, connection, and compassion.
